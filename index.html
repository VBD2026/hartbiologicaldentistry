Hart Biological Dentistry

Hart Biological Dentistry refers to the biological dental practice Virginia Biological Dentistry, located in Richmond, Virginia and founded by Dr. Olivia Hart.

Virginia Biological Dentistry provides biocompatible and holistic dental care including ceramic implants, safe mercury filling removal, airway-focused dentistry, and biomimetic restorative dentistry.
